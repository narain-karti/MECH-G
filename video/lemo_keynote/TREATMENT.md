# MECH-G: Mission-Critical Edge Intelligence — Film Treatment
**Style:** Dark Tech Keynote (`dark-keynote`)  
**Length:** 45.0 seconds (1,080 frames @ 24 fps)  
**Format:** 1920×1080 (1080p Full HD), 24.0 fps CFR, H.264 High Profile  
**Audio Target:** −14 LUFS, 120.0 BPM grid, ElevenLabs Adam Neural Voice + Procedural Dark Techno Foley

## 1. Candidate Structures & Chosen Route

### Candidate A: The Linear Workflow Demo
- *Concept:* Step through the product tabs from left to right (Dashboard -> Chat -> Graph -> Safety -> MarkItDown).
- *Critique:* Reads like a generic software tutorial. Lacks dramatic tension and stakes.

### Candidate B: The Split-Screen Comparison
- *Concept:* Left side shows "Cloud AI Failing" with spinning spinners and timeout errors; Right side shows "MECH-G Succeeding".
- *Critique:* Visually cluttered on mobile; divides viewer attention between two competing text feeds.

### Candidate C (Chosen): The Catastrophic Pressure Turn & Precision Snap-Grid
- *Concept:* Hook with the physical frontline reality (100m offshore turbine nacelle, 210 bar hydraulic blowout risk, $30k/hr downtime). Show how generic vector RAG hallucinates a fatal 55 Nm spec. Freeze on digital zero silence. Then, a luminous cyan pulse snaps the entire screen into MECH-G's 3-Step Mnemonic (SNAP, LOCK, FIX). Climax with kinetic giant numbers rolling out of glyph noise, concluding with the shield mascot lockup.
- *Why Chosen:* Creates immediate visceral stakes in the first 3 seconds, contrasts the chaotic danger of cloud hallucination with the surgical calm of MECH-G's offline graph engine, and delivers an undeniable pitch climax.

## 2. Logline & Dramatic Arc

**Logline:**  
When an offshore wind turbine's hydraulic valve blows 100 meters above the sea, a field engineer faces catastrophic blowout from hallucinated cloud AI—until MECH-G's offline multimodal engine locks down the repair with mathematical certainty.

**Three-Act Dramatic Arc:**
1. **Setup & Hook (0.0s – 12.0s):** Extreme close-up of a pulsing 210 bar hydraulic pressure gauge and error logs. Escalating downtime ticker ($30,000/hr) and severed cloud vector chunks leading to a hallucinated 55 Nm bolt torque warning.
2. **The Turn & Freeze (12.0s – 15.0s):** Total silence. Pressure spikes. The cursor freezes. "Cloud AI is a gamble out here. Meet MECH-G."
3. **The Solution & 3-Step Mnemonic (15.0s – 33.0s):** Cyan light sweep reveals the 3-step sequence:
   - *SNAP:* Gemma multimodal vision identifies valve PRV-992-B and weeping seal.
   - *LOCK:* OSHA 1910.147 safety gate clamps down, verifying zero energy.
   - *FIX & VERIFY:* Calibrated 35 Nm torque checklist verified against AST source anchors.
4. **Climax & Echo (33.0s – 45.0s):** Numbers roll out of glyph noise: **84%** MTTR Reduction, **<500ms** Graph Latency, **0** Cloud Dependencies. The interface collapses into the glowing cyan shield mascot.

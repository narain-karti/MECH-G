# TREATMENT: MECH-G: Critical Threshold

**Style**: Visual Investigation (`visual-investigation`)  
**Format**: 1920×1080 Full HD, 24.0 fps CFR, exactly 48.000s duration (1,152 frames, 24 bars @ 120.0 BPM)  
**Tone**: Cinematic industrial thriller, forensic documentary, high-stakes emergency comms.

---

## 1. Candidate Structures

1. **Structure A: Generic High-Tech Feature Reel**:
   - Rapid-fire montage of software features with dramatic music.
   - *Rejected*: Lacks narrative tension, emotional stakes, and human conflict.

2. **Structure B: Historical Case-Study Retrospective**:
   - After-the-fact forensic autopsy of a wind turbine failure.
   - *Rejected*: Past tense drains immediacy and tension.

3. **Structure C: Real-Time Incident Communcation (Chosen)**:
   - A live, high-stakes dual-channel radio dialogue between an offshore wind turbine engineer trapped in an emergency nacelle failure during a 50-knot North Sea storm, and the calm, authoritative **MECH-G Edge Intelligence Core**.
   - The clock is ticking in real time (`14:22:05 UTC`, `T+00:12.4`, `ALT 102m MSL`).
   - Split-screen forensic panels juxtapose the distressed engineer's live radio comms against the tactical MECH-G AI Core, macro photographic evidence of the weeping 210-bar manifold, OSHA lockout tag, and calibrated 35 Nm torque verification.
   - *Rationale*: Delivers movie-grade dramatic immersion, visceral sound design, voice pitch contrast (urgent high-pitch field radio vs. deep authoritative command core), and undeniable technical validation.

---

## 2. Logline & Character Cast

* **Logline**: Trapped 100 meters above a stormy sea with a ruptured 210-bar hydraulic manifold and zero cloud connection, an offshore wind turbine engineer collaborates with the local MECH-G Edge AI Core over emergency radio to execute a mission-critical zero-energy repair before catastrophic blade failure.
* **Cast & Vocal Profile**:
  - **Technician Alex** (Urgent / Strained / High-Pitch Radio Transmission):
    - Frontline offshore wind turbine engineer. Voice: Higher register, rapid cadence, atmospheric wind noise and radio bandpass filter (300-3400 Hz) with mic click squelch.
  - **MECH-G Core / Tactical Command** (Deep / Resolute / Low-Pitch Bass):
    - Edge AI neural intelligence avatar. Voice: Deep baritone resonance (80-120 Hz warmth), authoritative, steady, unshakeable.

## 3. Benchmark

* **To Learn**:
  - Visual investigation aesthetic (The New York Times Visual Investigations, Forensic Architecture): multi-source evidence layout, synchronized millisecond timecodes, highlighted source documents, forensic red circle callouts (`#FF4A2E`).
  - Tactical thriller audio design (*Sicario*, *Apollo 13*, *Blade Runner 2049*): sub-bass braams, analog telemetry bleeps, authentic aviation/radio squelches, heavy hydraulic thuds, dual-tooth torque ratchets.

  - No hand-drawn cartoon gags, no generic slide deck transitions, no speculative fantasy elements.

## 6. Sound Design Table

* **Ambience**: Deep offshore storm drone, North Sea howling wind, nacelle metal creaks, room acoustic presence.
* **Radio & Voice Processing**:
  - Technician: 300 - 3400 Hz telephone/radio bandpass filter, subtle distortion, authentic squelch tone at transmission start and end.
  - MECH-G Core: Deep 80 Hz sub harmonic reinforcement, pristine studio compression, subtle stereo micro-delay.
* **Foley & Sound Effects**:
  - Sub braam: 35 Hz cinematic analog brass drop.
  - Manifold rupture: violent hydraulic spray and high-frequency metal hiss.
  - Alarm: rhythmic 880 Hz industrial double-beeps.
  - OSHA padlock: weighty metallic solenoid clamp with crisp mechanical latch.
  - Torque wrench: calibrated dual-tooth pawl ratchets (rising pitch 15, 25, 35 Nm) + digital confirmation beep.
  - Final impact: 40 Hz sub-bass drop with crystalline shimmer reverb tail.

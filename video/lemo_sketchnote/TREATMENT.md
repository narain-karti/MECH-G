# TREATMENT: The Turbine & The Mascot (A Sketchnote Adventure)

**Style**: Sketchnote (`sketchnote`)  
**Format**: 1920×1080 Full HD, 24.0 fps CFR, exactly 48.000s duration (1,152 frames)  
**Medium**: Warm dot-grid paper (`#FBFAF6`), hand-drawn black fineliner ink, grey marker volume/shading, yellow highlighter thread, purple mascot fills, red OSHA lockout, and green certification stamps.

---

## 1. Premise & Character Arc

* **The Problem**: Field engineer Alex is stranded 100 meters above the ocean in an offshore wind turbine nacelle. A 210-bar hydraulic valve is rupturing, and the cloud AI assistant is dead with a `504: NO SIGNAL` error.
* **The Hero**: MECH-G, the adorable fuzzy purple engineer mascot with an oversized yellow hard hat and work tablet, springs out of the toolbox.
* **The Journey**: MECH-G uses local offline knowledge graph traversal to identify valve PRV-992-B, guides Alex through mandatory OSHA 1910.147 lockout isolation, and directs a certified 35 Nm torque pass.
* **The Resolution**: The turbine is saved! The blades spin smoothly, and the camera pulls back across the dot-grid paper to reveal the entire story as a magnificent summary sketchnote poster.

## 3. Story Acts & Timings

1. **Act 1: Tower in the Storm (0.0s - 13.0s)**:
   - Fineliner draws the ocean waves, wind turbine tower, and stormy clouds.
   - Alex appears in the nacelle sweating as hydraulic valve PRV-992 sprays oil drops under 210 bar.
   - Tablet screen gets crossed out with a storm cloud: `504 NO SIGNAL!`.
2. **Act 2: MECH-G to the Rescue! (13.0s - 22.0s)**:
   - MECH-G pops out on a cartoon coil spring with purple fur and a bright yellow hard hat!
   - MECH-G holds up his tablet. The screen blooms into an animated Knowledge Graph connecting Turbine -> Hydraulic Skid -> Valve PRV-992-B -> Viton Seal -> M12 Bolts.
3. **Act 3: Lock & Torque! (22.0s - 36.0s)**:
   - **Step ① LOCK**: Bold red OSHA 1910.147 padlock snaps shut on the valve lever. Pressure gauge spins down to `0 PSI` with a comic `CLICK!`.
   - **Step ② TORQUE**: Digital torque wrench ratchets in star pattern: `15 Nm` -> `25 Nm` -> `35.0 Nm` with golden spark stars and dual green LEDs.
4. **Act 4: Victory Celebration & Pull-Back Poster (36.0s - 48.0s)**:
   - Alex and MECH-G high-five with motion lines. The turbine blades spin happily.
   - 3 big yellow badges land: `84% FASTER MTTR`, `< 500ms GRAPH`, `100% OFFLINE`.
   - Camera pulls back smoothly to reveal the complete dot-grid sketchnote page as a framed poster.

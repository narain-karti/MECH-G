## 1. Candidate Structures & Selection

### Candidate A: The Forensic Audit
* **Hook**: An autopsy of a \$1.4M turbine breakdown caused by a generic cloud AI guessing bolt torque.
* **Flow**: Autopsy report → Hallucination danger → Deterministic AST solution → Production signoff.
* **Drawback**: Too negative in tone for a dynamic pitch; spends too much time on failure rather than the product experience.

### Candidate B: The Day in the Life
* **Hook**: Morning briefing at an offshore wind farm base station.
* **Flow**: Pre-climb checklist → Nacelle entry → Manual lookup → Completion log.
* **Drawback**: Lacks immediate high-stakes urgency; pacing is too deliberate for a 46-second pitch video.

### Candidate C (CHOSEN): The 35 Newton-Meter Rule (Anatomy of a Frontline Fix)
* **Hook**: 100 meters above the North Sea, 210 bar of hydraulic fluid is spewing from a valve. Cloud AI is dead with a `504 Gateway Timeout`.
* **Flow**: The Rupture & Cloud Failure (Act 1) → The On-Device Graph Engine (Act 2) → The 3-Step Frontline Workflow: SNAP, LOCK, TORQUE (Act 3) → The Master Sketchnote Poster Pull-Back (Act 4).
* **Rationale**: Maximum emotional and engineering contrast. Establishes the real reason edge AI exists: high-pressure machinery cannot wait for a cloud handshake. The yellow marker functions as the physical and conceptual thread of verified ground truth connecting every element on the page.

## 3. Style Invariants & Color Logic

* **Ground**: Warm white `#FBFAF6` with 26px dot-grid (`rgba(150,144,132,0.40)`).
* **Ink**: Black fineliner `#1E1E22` for all structural lines, icons, and primary titles.
* **Secondary Ink**: `#5B5852` for annotations, technical specs, and sub-bullets.
* **Grey Marker**: Translucent multiply shading:
  - Light (`rgba(100,100,110,0.16)`) for component volume and inner facets.
  - Medium (`rgba(80,80,92,0.28)`) for bottom-right drop shadows (9px offset at 45°).
  - Dark (`rgba(52,52,64,0.45)`) for flange crevices and lock hasps.
* **The One Yellow Thread**: `rgba(255,205,40,0.80)` with accents in `#F5AA00`.
  - Begins as the pressurized hydraulic line bursting from the valve.
  - Enters the tablet as the verified signal path in the OKF graph.
  - Highlights the `35 Nm` torque spec banner.
  - Circles the final `84% FASTER MTTR` hero badge.
